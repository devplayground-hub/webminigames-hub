❓ How to Play – Tic-Tac-Toe

Classic

  • Play on a 3×3 grid.
  • Take turns placing X or O.
  • Get 3 in a row, column, or diagonal to win.
  • Full board, no winner = Draw.
  • In PvComputer, you play against the computer.
  • No move for 10 seconds → a random cell is picked.

Vanishing

  • Your moves disappear after 5 turns.
  • Plan your moves carefully.
  • Costs 40 🪙 to unlock.

Ultimate

  • Play on 9 small 3×3 boards.
  • Your move decides where your opponent plays next.
  • Win 3 small boards in a row, column, or diagonal to win.
